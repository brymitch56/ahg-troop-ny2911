SMS Notification Program

American Heritage Girls Troop NY2911 texts parents and guardians who have opted in when their daughter is still checked in after a troop meeting or event ends. Documents for the program:

  • SMS Privacy Policy
  • SMS Terms & Conditions
  • Pickup Authorization & SMS Consent form (PDF) — blank specimen (image)

Opting in is optional and never a condition of participation. Reply STOP to any message to cancel, HELP for help. Questions: ahgtroopny2911@outlook.com.
